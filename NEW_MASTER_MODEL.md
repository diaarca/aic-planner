# Mathematical Model: Stage 2 Regional Master Problem

This document provides the detailed mathematical programming formulation for the **Regional Master Planning Problem** (Stage 2) of the AIC Planner. 

In the two-stage candidate-generation framework, the Master Problem optimizes product portfolio selection, manufacturing rates, factory instance counts, fuel battery power balancing, and 2D spatial bin packing across regional industrial zones (Areas), parameterized by the successfully routed candidate blueprints generated in Stage 1. This stage has no feedback loop to improve the blueprint portfolio; global factory-design optimality is not claimed.

---

## 1. Sets and Indices

### 1.1 Region and Resources
- $r \in R$: the region under consideration.
- $m \in M_r$: raw minerals extractable in region $r$.
- $fu \in FU_r \subseteq P_r$: battery fuels producible in region $r$.
- $fa \in FA_r$: facility types available in region $r$.
- $a \in A_r$: designated factory areas (PAC zones) within region $r$.

### 1.2 Products and Blueprints
- $p \in P_r$: producible end products in region $r$.
  Each product $p$ is associated with a precomputed, verified feasible factory blueprint from Stage 1.

---

## 2. Parameters and Data

### 2.1 Regional Infrastructure
- $bp_r$: base electrical power available in the region $r$.
- $s_r$: regional inventory storage limit per commodity item in region $r$.
- $T_{overflow} = 48 \text{ hours}: fixed time limit before accepting production overflow regional storage $s_r$.
- $l_m$: regional extraction / supply limit for mineral $m$ (units per minute).

### 2.2 Fuel and Power Specifications
- $p_{fu}$: electrical power generated by one simultaneously active fuel unit $fu$.
- $d_{fu}$: burn duration of one fuel unit $fu$ before exhaustion (seconds).
- $p_{fa}$: electrical power consumption of facility type $fa$.
- $fa_a^{fa}$: fixed number of infrastructure facilities $fa \in \{\text{zipline}, \text{defense}, \text{mining\_rig}\}$ operating in area $a$.

### 2.3 Blueprint-Derived Factory Parameters (from Stage 1)
For each producible product $p \in P_r$, Stage 1 supplies candidate-derived parameters:
- $v_p$: market economic value of product $p$ (stock bills per unit).
- $T_p$: production cycle duration for one product unit batch (seconds).
- $m_p^m$: exact quantity of mineral $m$ consumed per unit batch of product $p$.
- $fa_p^{fa}$: total count of facility type $fa$ contained inside one factory blueprint of product $p$.
- $P_p^{factory}$: total electrical power required to operate one factory of product $p$:
  $$P_p^{factory} = \sum_{fa \in FA_r} fa_p^{fa} \cdot p_{fa}$$
- $w_p$: bounding box width of the factory blueprint (tiles).
- $h_p$: bounding box height of the factory blueprint (tiles).
- $A_p = w_p \cdot h_p$: footprint area of the factory blueprint ($\text{tiles}^2$).
- $d_p$: depot docking interface length required by the factory blueprint (tiles), where $d_p = 3 \cdot fa_p^{\text{unloader}}$.

### 2.4 Area Topography and Docking Infrastructure
For each industrial area $a \in A_r$:
- $W_a$: PAC width (tiles).
- $H_a$: PAC height (tiles).
- $\mathcal{D}_a$: set of static unloader depot segments along the area boundary:
  $$\mathcal{D}_a = \mathcal{D}_a^{top} \cup \mathcal{D}_a^{right} \cup \mathcal{D}_a^{bottom} \cup \mathcal{D}_a^{left}$$
  where each depot segment defines valid alignment coordinates along the boundary.
- $L_a^{depot}$: total aggregate static depot length available in area $a$ (tiles).

---

## 3. Decision Variables

### 3.1 Production and Operational Variables
- $produce_p \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$: net production rate of product $p$ (units per minute).
- $factory_p^a \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$: number of factory instances for product $p$ placed in area $a$.
- $active_{fu} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$: number of simultaneously discharging fuel batteries of type $fu$.

### 3.2 2D Area Placement Variables (Slot Formulation)
To enforce exact 2D non-overlapping bin packing and depot alignment within each area $a \in A_r$, we introduce a set of placement slots $k \in \{1, \dots, K_a\}$. 

The slot capacity $K_a$ is not an arbitrary fixed constant; it is dynamically bounded based on the dimensions and depot demand of the smallest candidate blueprint among all producible products:
$$K_a = \min \left( \left\lfloor \frac{W_a \cdot H_a}{\min_{p \in P_r} A_p} \right\rfloor, \; \left\lfloor \frac{L_a^{depot}}{\min_{p \in P_r} d_p} \right\rfloor \right)$$
where:
- $\min_{p \in P_r} A_p$ is the minimum bounding box area across all candidate blueprints.
- $\min_{p \in P_r} d_p$ is the minimum depot docking interface length required across all candidate blueprints.
- If $\max(W_a, H_a) < \min_{p} \min(w_p, h_p)$ or $L_a^{depot} = 0$, then $K_a = 0$.

For each slot $k \in \{1, \dots, K_a\}$:
- $active_{a, k} \in \{0, 1\}$: binary indicator that slot $k$ in area $a$ is occupied by a factory.
- $prod_{a, k, p} \in \{0, 1\}$: binary indicator that slot $k$ hosts product factory $p$.
- $rot_{a, k, r} \in \{0, 1\}$ for $r \in \{0^\circ, 90^\circ, 180^\circ, 270^\circ\}$: orientation of the factory in slot $k$.
- $bx_{a, k} \in [0, W_a]$: X-coordinate of slot $k$'s bottom-left corner.
- $by_{a, k} \in [0, H_a]$: Y-coordinate of slot $k$'s bottom-left corner.
- $bw_{a, k} \in [0, W_a]$: effective placed width of slot $k$.
- $bh_{a, k} \in [0, H_a]$: effective placed height of slot $k$.

---

## 4. Objective Function

The objective is to **maximize the net economic revenue per minute**, defined as the value of all manufactured goods produced minus the economic opportunity cost of batteries burned for electrical power:

$$\max \quad \sum_{p \in P_r} \left( produce_p \cdot v_p \right) - \sum_{fu \in FU_r} \left( active_{fu} \cdot \frac{60}{d_{fu}} \cdot v_{fu} \right)$$

---

## 5. Constraints

### 5.1 Fuel Self-Sufficiency (Burn Balance)
The number of fuel units burned per minute cannot exceed the rate of fuel units produced:
$$produce_{fu} - active_{fu} \cdot \frac{60}{d_{fu}} \ge 0 \quad \forall fu \in FU_r$$
If a fuel item $fu \in FU_r$ is not produced ($produce_{fu} = 0$), no batteries of that type can be active:
$$active_{fu} = 0 \quad \forall fu \in FU_r : fu \notin P_r$$

### 5.2 Regional Storage Limit (Anti-Overflow)
To avoid overflowing the regional warehouse within 48 hours ($2880$ minutes):
- For non-fuel products:
  $$produce_p \le \frac{s_r}{2880} \quad \forall p \in P_r \setminus FU_r$$
- For fuel products (accounting for self-consumption):
  $$produce_{fu} - active_{fu} \cdot \frac{60}{d_{fu}} \le \frac{s_r}{2880} \quad \forall fu \in FU_r$$

### 5.3 Regional Mineral Availability
The aggregate mineral consumption rate across all active production lines cannot exceed the regional extraction limit $l_m$:
$$\sum_{p \in P_r} \left( produce_p \cdot m_p^m \right) \le l_m \quad \forall m \in M_r$$

### 5.4 Production Capacity Linking
Each factory of product $p$ completes one batch of 1 unit every $T_p$ seconds ($\frac{60}{T_p}$ units/minute). The aggregate manufacturing capacity across all areas must cover the target production rate:
$$produce_p \le \sum_{a \in A_r} \left( factory_p^a \cdot \frac{60}{T_p} \right) \quad \forall p \in P_r$$

### 5.5 Regional Electrical Power Balance
Total regional power demand (base infrastructure demand plus manufacturing factory demand) must not exceed total regional generation (regional base power plus active battery discharge):
$$\underbrace{\sum_{a \in A_r} \sum_{fa} \left( fa_a^{fa} \cdot p_{fa} \right)}_{\text{Fixed Area Infrastructure Power}} + \underbrace{\sum_{p \in P_r} \sum_{a \in A_r} \left( factory_p^a \cdot P_p^{factory} \right)}_{\text{Manufacturing Factory Power}} \le bp_r + \underbrace{\sum_{fu \in FU_r} \left( active_{fu} \cdot p_{fu} \right)}_{\text{Battery Power Generation}}$$

### 5.6 Exact 2D Spatial Packing and Depot Alignment (within Areas)
For each area $a \in A_r$ where 2D blueprints are active:

1. **Slot Product Assignment**:
   $$\sum_{p \in P_r} prod_{a, k, p} = active_{a, k} \quad \forall k \in \{1, \dots, K_a\}$$

2. **Factory Count Consistency**:
   $$factory_p^a = \sum_{k=1}^{K_a} prod_{a, k, p} \quad \forall p \in P_r$$

3. **Orientation and Boundary Depot Availability**:
   $$\sum_{r \in \{0^\circ, 90^\circ, 180^\circ, 270^\circ\}} rot_{a, k, r} = active_{a, k} \quad \forall k$$
   A factory orientation is permitted only if area $a$ possesses a static depot on the corresponding perimeter edge:
   $$rot_{a, k, 0} = 0 \quad \text{if } \mathcal{D}_a^{top} = \emptyset$$
   $$rot_{a, k, 90} = 0 \quad \text{if } \mathcal{D}_a^{right} = \emptyset$$
   $$rot_{a, k, 180} = 0 \quad \text{if } \mathcal{D}_a^{bottom} = \emptyset$$
   $$rot_{a, k, 270} = 0 \quad \text{if } \mathcal{D}_a^{left} = \emptyset$$

4. **Effective Placed Dimensions**:
   Let $\text{is\_pr}_{a, k, p, r} = prod_{a, k, p} \land rot_{a, k, r}$. Then:
   $$bw_{a, k} = \sum_{p \in P_r} \left( \sum_{r \in \{0, 180\}} \text{is\_pr}_{a, k, p, r} \cdot w_p + \sum_{r \in \{90, 270\}} \text{is\_pr}_{a, k, p, r} \cdot h_p \right)$$
   $$bh_{a, k} = \sum_{p \in P_r} \left( \sum_{r \in \{0, 180\}} \text{is\_pr}_{a, k, p, r} \cdot h_p + \sum_{r \in \{90, 270\}} \text{is\_pr}_{a, k, p, r} \cdot w_p \right)$$

5. **Area Boundary Containment**:
   $$bx_{a, k} + bw_{a, k} \le W_a \quad \forall k$$
   $$by_{a, k} + bh_{a, k} \le H_a \quad \forall k$$

6. **Flush Static Depot Alignment**:
   When orientation $r$ is chosen, the unloader dock edge of the factory blueprint must align flush with the static depot boundary:
   - For $r = 0^\circ$ (top depot):
     $$by_{a, k} + bh_{a, k} = H_a, \quad bx_{a, k} \ge x_1^{top}, \quad bx_{a, k} + bw_{a, k} - 1 \le x_2^{top}$$
   - For $r = 90^\circ$ (right depot):
     $$bx_{a, k} + bw_{a, k} = W_a, \quad by_{a, k} \ge y_1^{right}, \quad by_{a, k} + bh_{a, k} - 1 \le y_2^{right}$$
   - For $r = 180^\circ$ (bottom depot):
     $$by_{a, k} = 0, \quad bx_{a, k} \ge x_1^{bot}, \quad bx_{a, k} + bw_{a, k} - 1 \le x_2^{bot}$$
   - For $r = 270^\circ$ (left depot):
     $$bx_{a, k} = 0, \quad by_{a, k} \ge y_1^{left}, \quad by_{a, k} + bh_{a, k} - 1 \le y_2^{left}$$

7. **Pairwise 2D Non-Overlapping Rectangle Packing**:
   Define horizontal and vertical optional interval variables for each slot:
   $$I_{a, k}^x = \text{Interval}(bx_{a, k}, bw_{a, k}, bx_{a, k} + bw_{a, k}) \quad \text{active if } active_{a, k}$$
   $$I_{a, k}^y = \text{Interval}(by_{a, k}, bh_{a, k}, by_{a, k} + bh_{a, k}) \quad \text{active if } active_{a, k}$$
   Enforce mutual non-overlap across all slots in area $a$:
   $$\text{NoOverlap2D}\left( \{(I_{a, k}^x, I_{a, k}^y) : k \in \{1, \dots, K_a\}\} \right)$$

8. **Symmetry Breaking**:
   $$active_{a, k} \ge active_{a, k+1} \quad \forall k \in \{1, \dots, K_a - 1\}$$

## Implementation bounds and precision

The current implementation requires a routed candidate for every product passed to the regional solver. It has no aggregate-area fallback. It supports one continuous depot segment per edge. The slot bound is computed from candidate bounding areas and positive depot demands; a region with no usable docking capacity has zero slots.

Production is integer items/minute. Factory domains are bounded by spatial and docking capacity. Gross fuel production is bounded by factory throughput; the inventory bound applies after subtracting fuel burned for power. The 48-hour inventory horizon is fixed. Consumption and per-unit cycle-time coefficients are scaled by one million, rounded upward; resource budgets are rounded downward. This avoids silently truncating fractional consumption. Prices have up to three decimal places, and integer-second fuel durations use bounded common-denominator scaling. `OPTIMAL` refers to this candidate-restricted model and conservative numerical representation. Runtime status is reported without converting feasible or unknown outcomes into optimality claims.

## Search budget and symmetry

Slots within an area are interchangeable. The implementation orders them by product/orientation group and orders identical groups along their depot edge; this removes equivalent permutations without excluding distinct physical packings. Inactive slot origins are fixed to zero. The regional search defaults to 30 seconds, configurable with `--solve-seconds`; a found incumbent without an optimality proof is reported as `FEASIBLE`.
